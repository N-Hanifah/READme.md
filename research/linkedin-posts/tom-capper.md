We're all wrestling with the transformation of SEO, although you wouldn't know it from Google's official advice, which looks the same as it always did. This seemed disingenuous in the past, but now it seems tone deaf as well. Meanwhile, the funnel is taking a beating, SEO jobs are looking for AI skills, and AI skills are looking for SEO jobs. There are good news stories in all this though - what if writing for AI is actually better writing than the SEO-centric content of the past?
