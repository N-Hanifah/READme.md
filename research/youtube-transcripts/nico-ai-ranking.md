## Key Insights

### The Three Layers of Modern Search Optimization

| Term | Definition | Role |
|---|---|---|
| SEO | Search Engine Optimization — on-site, technical, and offsite techniques | Base layer, foundational for all search presence |
| AEO | Answer Engine Optimization — optimizing for featured snippets and instant answers | Allows you to become the direct answer provider |
| GEO | Generative Engine Optimization — optimizing for AI search engines to get cited | Advanced layer ensuring AI engines cite and favor your content |

### Evolution of Search
- **2015:** Google introduced featured snippets
- **2022:** ChatGPT launched, shifting search behavior
- **2024:** Google rolled out AI Overviews
- **2026:** Estimated 80–93% of searches result in zero clicks

### Why SEO Still Matters
76% of AI overview citations come from the top 10 SEO-ranked pages. Without solid SEO, AEO and GEO are ineffective.

### Three Core SEO Fundamentals
| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| On-site SEO | Content quality, site structure, conversion rates |
| Technical SEO | Schema, URL structure, crawlability |
| Offsite SEO | Backlinks, brand mentions, social profiles |

### Capsule Content Technique (for GEO)
Each piece of content should stand alone as a coherent answer if extracted separately. Blog headings (H2) should pose a question, immediately followed by a concise direct answer.

- **Bad:** "In today's digital landscape, businesses seek ways to improve…"
- **Good:** "Technical SEO is the process of optimizing your website infrastructure so that search engines can crawl, index, and rank your pages effectively."

### Effort Distribution
- SEO accounts for ~70% of effort and success
- Remaining 30%: capsule content, proper page structure, multi-platform diversification

---

## Key Takeaway
SEO gets you found → AEO helps you become the answer → GEO gets you recommended by AI. All three layers are sequential and interdependent.
