## Key Insights

### SEO Remains Vital Despite AI Evolution
- Google still processes billions of searches daily with 20% growth last year
- Google drives 190x more traffic to websites compared to ChatGPT
- AI assistants rely on traditional search indexes — SEO is foundational, not obsolete

### Search Intent: The Most Important SEO Concept
| Intent Type | Description | Buyer Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Informational | Wants knowledge/answers | Early (learning) |
| Commercial | Comparing options | Mid-funnel |
| Transactional | Ready to buy | Bottom funnel |
| Navigational | Looking for specific brand | Branded search |

### Keyword Sweet Spot Framework
| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| Demand | Is the keyword actually searched? |
| Fit | Does it align with your funnel stage? |
| Intent | Can you satisfy the searcher's needs? |
| Difficulty | Can your site realistically rank? |

### Content Creation Priorities (Bottom-Up Funnel)
1. Money Pages — product/service/pricing pages (transactional)
2. Build One Cluster — related topic clusters around money pages
3. Repeat Clustering — finish one cluster before starting another

### SEO in the Age of AI Search
- AI assistants synthesize and cite sources based on authority, relevance, and clarity
- SEO foundations work for AI visibility: authoritative content, clear structure, schema markup, quality backlinks
- Doing SEO right IS AI optimization

### 30-Day Action Plan
| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation — GSC setup, keyword research, intent analysis |
| 2 | Money Page — write/publish most important page |
| 3 | First Funnel Cluster — publish supporting content with internal links |
| 4 | Promote & Monitor — journalist queries, AI visibility check, GSC monitoring |

## Key Takeaway
SEO is the foundation of AI search, not its rival. Understanding searcher needs, producing excellent content, and earning trust drives visibility on both Google and AI assistants.
