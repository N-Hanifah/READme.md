## Key Insights

### Skill 1: Proving the Value of AI SEO through Measurement
"If you can't measure it, you can't sell or scale it."

Four key AI SEO metrics to track:
| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Market Weighted Coverage | Whether your brand is mentioned in AI answers across platforms, weighted by platform importance |
| Position | The rank where your brand appears in AI answers (aim for top 3) |
| Share of Voice | Combines coverage and position, adjusted for number of competitors |
| Brand Coverage in Citations | Tracks if your brand appears in actual sources cited by AI |

### Skill 2: Controlling the Brand Narrative
AI systems mostly reflect what's available online. Key actions:
- Identify the 5–10 critical platforms in your niche by extracting AI citations
- Do SEO on those profiles, not just create them
- Build review diversity across niche-specific platforms
- Ensure NAPW consistency (Name, Address, Phone, Website)

### Skill 3: Building a Knowledge Base
Upload business docs, messaging, FAQs, case studies, and policies to Claude or ChatGPT Projects to guide AI output reliably. This significantly improves on-brand output quality.

### Skill 4: Creating Effective AI-Assisted SEO Content
- Use knowledge base to generate prompts tailored to brand specifics
- Incorporate human review for quality assurance and expert tone
- Add real-world proof (original data, screenshots, experiences)
- Key rule: "If AI can easily create it, don't publish it."

### Skill 5: Link Building 2.0 (Brand Mention Focus)
| Aspect | Traditional | AI SEO |
|---|---|---|
| Link Type | Follow links for SEO juice | Link type less important; any brand mention helps |
| Goal | Increase authority and rankings | Increase brand trust with AI retrieval sources |

### Skill 6: AI Coding Skill
Coding with AI is a high-leverage skill enabling rapid SEO and site improvements. Small teams can deliver big impact by directing AI for coding tasks.

Mastering these 6 AI SEO skills allows brands to dominate AI search environments, generate more revenue, and scale effectively.
