## Article 1
**Title:** GSC's New AI Overview Reporting – How Can We Use This Information?
**URL:** https://www.mariehaynes.com/new-aio-and-aimode-info-in-gsc/
**Date:** June 2026

**Summary:**
Google is now providing data in Search Console showing how content is being used in AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Discover. The new "Generative AI" report under Performance shows impressions within these features, filterable by pages, countries, devices, and dates.

**Key insights:**
- The CMA (UK) pushed Google for better attribution and transparency for publishers
- Publishers now have a toggle in GSC to opt out of AI features
- Marie recommends staying opted in and using the data to improve content strategy
- Cross-reference high-impression pages in AI Overviews with standard click data to identify what makes content worth clicking beyond the AI answer
- Focus on non-commodity content: first-hand experience, original images/research, deeper dives than AI summaries can offer
